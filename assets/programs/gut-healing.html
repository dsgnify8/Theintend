The Intend

This program is intended for general wellness support and is not a substitute for individual medical advice. If coeliac disease has not been ruled out, ask your doctor about testing before removing gluten — results become unreliable once gluten is withdrawn. Blood in the stool, unexplained weight loss, persistent vomiting, difficulty swallowing, or a change in bowel habit lasting more than a few weeks need medical assessment rather than a supplement protocol. Oregano oil, neem, and wormwood are potent antimicrobials and are not intended for indefinite use.
