Throughout the Program

Lifestyle & nutrition

Scroll sideways →

  • Follow a 16:8 intermittent fasting schedule whenever appropriate
  • Eat 2–3 balanced meals daily and avoid frequent snacking
  • Follow a gluten-free diet throughout the program
  • Avoid cow’s milk and dairy products, though goat and sheep milk products are allowed in moderation if well tolerated
  • Consume adequate high-quality protein to help preserve lean muscle mass
  • Minimize sugar, sweets, sweetened beverages, fruit juices, and ultra-processed foods
  • Avoid refined carbohydrates, white flour products, pastries, white bread, white rice, and sugary snacks
  • Focus on vegetables, lean proteins, healthy fats, nuts, seeds, legumes, and low-glycemic foods
  • Drink 2–3 liters of water daily
  • Engage in regular resistance training combined with cardiovascular exercise
  • Aim for 7–9 hours of quality sleep each night
Terramica

This program is intended for general wellness support and is not a substitute for individual medical advice. Retatrutide is an investigational compound that has not been approved by regulators for general use, and Tesamorelin is prescription-only. GLP-1-class agents commonly cause nausea and vomiting, carry a risk of gallbladder disease and pancreatitis, and are not appropriate in pregnancy or for anyone with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2. Take iodine only after thyroid function has been assessed. If you take glucose-lowering medication, review this program with your physician first: 16:8 fasting alongside berberine, chromium, and alpha lipoic acid can lower blood sugar further than intended.
