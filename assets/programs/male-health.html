Testosterone support & hormonal optimization

The Intend

This program is intended for general wellness support and is not a substitute for individual medical advice. Testosterone therapy is prescription-only and requires baseline and ongoing bloodwork, including PSA. Yohimbe and PT-141 can raise blood pressure and interact with cardiovascular and psychiatric medication — review both with your physician before use. Any new urinary symptoms or a rising PSA should be assessed by a doctor rather than managed with supplements.
