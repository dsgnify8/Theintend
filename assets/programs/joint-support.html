03

Pillar Two

Lifestyle

Daily movement is essential for joint recovery.

Recommended activities
  • Walking 30–60 minutes daily
  • Gentle mobility exercises
  • Circular range-of-motion exercises for affected joints
  • Stretching
  • Light resistance training
  • Bodyweight exercises within pain-free range
  • Swimming or water exercises
Rest & repair

Aim for 7–9 hours of quality sleep each night, as tissue repair occurs primarily during deep sleep.

Avoid prolonged immobilization unless specifically advised by your physician.

Keep in Mind

Additional recommendations

  • 01Maintain healthy body weight to reduce mechanical stress on joints.
  • 02Prioritize an anti-inflammatory lifestyle.
  • 03Stay physically active while avoiding sharp pain.
  • 04Consider physiotherapy, massage therapy, mobility work, and rehabilitation exercises.
  • 05Recovery is most effective when nutrition, movement, supplementation, and peptide therapy are combined consistently.
