Muscle Growth · Metabolic Optimization · Regeneration

This comprehensive protocol is designed to support lean muscle growth, improve insulin sensitivity, enhance mitochondrial function, optimize energy metabolism, support recovery of muscles and joints, and improve body composition during insulin resistance. The program combines peptide therapy, targeted nutrition, metabolic supplementation, herbal support, mushroom extracts, resistance training, and lifestyle optimization.

The Daily Rhythm

Eating structure

Two meals per day without unnecessary snacks, and a minimum sixteen-hour fasting period between the last meal of the day and the first meal of the following day.

The fasting window supports improved insulin sensitivity, better metabolic flexibility, reduced insulin spikes, and cellular repair processes.

The Intend

This program is intended for general wellness support and is not a substitute for individual medical advice. If you take metformin, a sulfonylurea, or insulin, review this protocol with your physician before starting: a 16-hour daily fast combined with berberine, chromium, and alpha-lipoic acid can lower blood sugar further than intended. CJC-1295 with Ipamorelin and PEG-MGF act on growth pathways and warrant medical screening, particularly alongside insulin resistance. All peptides listed here are prohibited in competitive sport under WADA rules.
