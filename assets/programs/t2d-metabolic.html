The Intend

This program is intended for general wellness support and is not a substitute for individual medical advice. If you are taking glucose-lowering medication such as insulin, sulfonylureas, or metformin, speak with your physician before starting — extended fasting combined with glucose-lowering botanicals can cause blood sugar to fall too low. Monitor your levels and do not stop or adjust prescribed medication on your own.
