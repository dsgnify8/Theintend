Timing

The testing window

The female hormone panel must be drawn on day 2 or day 3 of the menstrual cycle. Timing is not optional here — FSH, LH, and estradiol are only interpretable against an early-follicular baseline, so a sample taken later in the cycle cannot be read the same way.

Everything else is untimed. The general and thyroid panels can be drawn on any day of the cycle.

Pillar Three

Lifestyle

  • Consume a high-protein, nutrient-dense diet
  • Include healthy fats daily
  • Perform resistance training 3–4 times per week
  • Walk daily
  • Sleep 7–8 hours every night
  • Manage stress effectively
  • Maintain a healthy body composition
  • Limit sugar, ultra-processed foods, and alcohol
  • Monitor hormone and thyroid function every 6–12 months, or as recommended by your healthcare provider
The Intend

This program is intended for general wellness support and is not a substitute for individual medical advice. Vitex, DIM, red clover, wild yam, and evening primrose all act on hormone pathways: they are not appropriate in pregnancy or while breastfeeding, can interfere with hormonal contraception and fertility treatment, and should be reviewed with a doctor by anyone with a history of hormone-sensitive cancer. Take iodine only after thyroid function has been assessed, as noted in the formulary. AMH and FSH results are best interpreted by a clinician alongside your history rather than read in isolation.
