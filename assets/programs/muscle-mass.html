Protocol

Muscle Growth · Recovery · Testosterone Support · Performance

This comprehensive protocol is designed to support lean muscle mass development, improve recovery after training, enhance mitochondrial function, optimize anabolic pathways, support healthy testosterone balance, improve performance, and protect joints and connective tissues. The program combines peptide therapy, resistance training, targeted nutrition, supplementation, herbal medicine, mushroom support, and lifestyle optimization.

The Intend

This program is intended for general wellness support and is not a substitute for individual medical advice. IGF-1 LR3, PEG-MGF, and CJC-1295 with Ipamorelin act on growth pathways: IGF-1 in particular can lower blood sugar sharply, and growth-promoting agents warrant medical screening before use, especially with any personal or family history of cancer or diabetes. All peptides listed here are prohibited in competitive sport under WADA rules. Review the full protocol with a physician before starting.
