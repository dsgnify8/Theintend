The Intend

This program is intended for general wellness support and is not a substitute for individual medical advice. Acne that is cystic, painful, or leaving marks should be assessed by a dermatologist, because scarring is permanent once it forms but largely preventable with timely treatment, and a supplement protocol is not a substitute for that. LL-37 is a cathelicidin, and elevated cathelicidin activity is implicated in rosacea, so anyone with rosacea or rosacea-like flushing should raise it with their clinician before use. All IV therapies listed, including ozone, must be administered by qualified medical staff. If pregnant, breastfeeding, or taking hormonal contraception, review the hormonal balance stack with a doctor first.
